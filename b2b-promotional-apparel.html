01Project overview

For four years I managed the marketing and B2B supply of custom-printed apparel: T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies, caps, staff uniforms and event merchandise for restaurants, agencies, and corporate, bulk and export buyers. Marketing brought the enquiries; the rest of the job was turning them into orders delivered on time and on spec.

02Business background

B2B buyers order in bulk for staff, events and promotions. The price depends on quantity, fabric and the branding method, so almost every sale starts with a question and ends with a quotation.

03Challenge

  • Winning buyers without a big brand name behind me.
  • First-time bulk buyers who needed trust and clear pricing before ordering.
  • Enquiries that had to be qualified before I spent time on a quote.

04Objective

A steady flow of qualified enquiries, quotes that convert, and orders delivered on spec so buyers come back.

05My role

  • Lead generation: Meta click-to-WhatsApp ads, social media, cold emails and direct messages
  • Content: short product videos, digital catalogues, product creatives and descriptions
  • Sales: buyer qualification, quotations, quantity-based pricing and costing
  • Operations: sourcing, production coordination, printing and embroidery, quality checks, packaging and delivery
  • Relationships: enquiry follow-ups and client communication

06Strategy

Show the real product and its spec, then qualify before quoting. Quantity-based pricing and minimum orders were agreed upfront, so time went to buyers who could order.

07Execution

Sales-focused content across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn, digital catalogues for outreach, and click-to-WhatsApp ads that sent buyers straight to a conversation.

10Insights and learnings

Owners want qualified buyers at a cost that makes sense.

Running the business taught me to plan a campaign around what happens after the lead: the reply, the quote, the order and the reorder. I still ask about margins, minimum orders and who replies to leads before a client's first rupee is spent.
