01Project overview

Shoephoria, an imported-shoes store selling men's and women's styles in many colours, sold through Instagram. I handled its content, product photography and paid ads for three months.

02Business background

On Instagram, people browse a store's feed like a shop window. A wide range in many colours has to be easy to scan.

03Challenge

A wide range is hard to browse on Instagram. Each product had to be easy to notice, compare and enquire about.

04Objective

Make the profile work like a catalogue, and support enquiries and online sales with paid ads.

05My role

  • Product photoshoots and short videos
  • Content calendar and post planning
  • Branded creatives in Canva and CapCut
  • Captions with a clear way to order
  • Paid ads to support enquiries and online sales

06Strategy

Four recurring post types: single products, colour options, new arrivals and offers, across reels, carousels and stories.

07Execution

  • Photographed the shoes and edited product-focused visuals.
  • Designed branded creatives and wrote captions with a clear way to order.
  • Kept the feed consistent, so the profile works like a catalogue.
  • Ran paid ads to support enquiries and online sales.

08Tools and platforms

Product photographyCanvaCapCutInstagramMeta Ads

09Results

A consistent, catalogue-style feed where every product could be noticed, compared and ordered, with a repeatable system for new arrivals and offers.

10Insights and learnings

The goal was never to fill the feed.

It was to make each product easier to notice, understand and buy.
