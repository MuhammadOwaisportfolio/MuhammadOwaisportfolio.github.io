01Project overview

Influencio was our startup concept for the Managing the Entrepreneurial Venture course: a subscription platform where SMEs and agencies filter influencers by niche, audience and engagement, then manage campaigns without paying an agency in the middle. Six of us built it, and it was well reviewed at the final-year showcase.

02Business background

In Pakistan, much influencer marketing runs through managers and agencies, and small businesses pay for the access. Our report found that nano and micro creators, with 1K to 50K followers, are a segment other platforms often overlook.

03Challenge

Two complaints came up again and again in our survey: influencer managers who charge too much and aren't transparent, and how hard it is to find the right influencer. The platform had to solve both at a price a small business would pay.

04Objective

  • Find out whether SMEs and agencies want a self-serve influencer platform.
  • Size the market and the competition before building anything.
  • Design pricing a small business could afford.
  • Show the idea working in a prototype.

05My role

I was one of six team members. Together we produced:

  • The marketing intelligence report: industry overview, key players, Porter's Five Forces, a SWOT of two competitors and their marketing mix
  • A market survey of business owners and marketers
  • The subscription model and Year 1 projections
  • A website prototype, presented at the final-year showcase

06Strategy

Research before product. The report came first, and every design decision had to answer something it found.

07Execution

  • Ran the market survey with business owners and marketers.
  • Built three subscription plans, with Gold as the plan most respondents picked.
  • Designed the prototype around filters for niche, audience and engagement.
  • Modelled Year 1 costs and revenue.

09Results

The concept was well reviewed at the final-year showcase. It was a classroom project, so its numbers are a model, not a result.

When I checked the market again later, Pakistan already had several influencer platforms, and most charged per deal or were free in beta. A flat subscription would be a hard sell.

10Insights and learnings

Validate the market before the product.

The research found real pain, but pricing and competition decide whether a good idea becomes a business. If I pitched Influencio today, I'd pitch measured influencer marketing: verified performance data, fake-follower checks and a cost per result for every creator.
