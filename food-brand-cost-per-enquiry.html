01Project overview

A food brand ran click-to-WhatsApp campaigns on Meta to bring in enquiries from buyers. I managed and optimised the campaign at the agency.

02Business background

For a food brand, WhatsApp is the order desk: people message to ask about products, prices and delivery.

03Challenge

In the early days, each enquiry cost about PKR 49. Some ads kept spending without bringing enquiries, and single good or bad days made the picture noisy.

04Objective

Bring the cost per enquiry down as the campaign matured, and measure it against a number the client could plan on.

05My role

  • Campaign management and optimisation in Meta Ads Manager
  • Creative feedback: a clearer message and a visible price
  • Daily updates to the project manager and weekly reporting to the client

06Strategy

  1. 1
    Cut the waste
    Pause the ads that keep spending without bringing enquiries.
  2. 2
    Sharpen the message
    Make the message and the price clearer in the ads that stay.
  3. 3
    Be patient
    Let the campaign settle and assess it on 7-day totals, not single days.

07Execution

  • Paused ads that had spent about twice the average cost per result with no result.
  • Clarified the message and the price in the remaining creatives.
  • Let the campaign settle over several weeks instead of reacting to single days.
  • Reported the 7-day number, with the best day shown separately.

09Results

Over the last 7 days the campaign brought 148 enquiries at PKR 21.40 each, down 56% from about PKR 49 in the early days. The best single day came in at PKR 11.31.

10Insights and learnings

I report the 7-day number, not the best day.

One cheap day can make an ad look better than it is. The best day here was PKR 11.31, but the number I reported, and the one the client could plan on, was PKR 21.40.
