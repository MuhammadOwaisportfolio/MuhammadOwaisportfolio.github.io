What makes influencer marketing work? A study of 505 people.

Our final-year research tested which influencer traits drive engagement and buying intent. 505 social media users in Karachi answered a structured questionnaire, analysed with PLS-SEM in SmartPLS 4.

In short

In a study of 505 social media users in Karachi, an influencer's entertainment value (β 0.608) and expertise (β 0.263) raised engagement, while attractiveness lowered it (β −0.654). Engagement alone didn't create buying intent; expected value did.

01Project overview

A BBA research project at Iqra University with four co-authors. We asked which traits of an influencer make people engage with a brand, and whether that engagement turns into wanting to buy.

02Background

Brands often pick influencers on follower counts and looks. We wanted evidence on what actually moves an audience: expertise, trustworthiness, attractiveness, entertainment value or informativeness.

03Challenge

Engagement is easy to see and easy to over-value. The real question was whether likes and comments lead to buying intent, and which influencer traits drive each.

04Objective

  • Measure how five influencer traits affect engagement and expected value.
  • Test whether engagement, expected value and attitude predict purchase intention.
  • Turn the results into practical advice for choosing influencers.

05My role

I was one of five co-authors. The study ran in four stages:

  1. 1
    Model
    Five influencer traits, linked to engagement, expected value, attitude and purchase intention.
  2. 2
    Questionnaire
    Structured, on a 7-point scale.
  3. 3
    Sample
    505 social media users in Karachi, a convenience sample.
  4. 4
    Analysis
    PLS-SEM in SmartPLS 4.

06Research design

One model, tested as a whole. Each influencer trait had a path to engagement and to expected value, and those paths fed attitude and purchase intention.

07Execution

  • Built the questionnaire on a 7-point scale.
  • Collected 505 responses from social media users in Karachi; 56.6% were aged 18 to 27.
  • Analysed the model with PLS-SEM in SmartPLS 4.
  • Reported supported and rejected relationships from the results table.

10Insights and learnings

Choose influencers for expertise and entertainment, not looks.

Engagement alone didn't create buying intent; in our model it even pointed the other way. Expected value did, which means price, availability and a clear call to action. It's why I measure creator campaigns by cost per result, not likes.
