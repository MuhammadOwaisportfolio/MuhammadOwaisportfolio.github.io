01Project overview

An online T-shirt and casualwear brand asked for a Meta Ads plan. Its PKR 4–5 lakh of ad spend had brought roughly PKR 18–20 lakh in sales, a reported ROAS of about 3.5, which looked healthy.

02Business background

Then the brand shared its margin: 15% after delivery and all other costs. Some orders also came through WhatsApp, which the reported ROAS could not see.

03Challenge

Before planning a budget, every order had to be checked against what the ad cost to win it.

04Objective

Move the account toward break-even and beyond by improving cost per order and order value together.

05My role

  • Unit economics and the break-even analysis
  • A findings note for the client
  • An 8-week Meta plan with weekly ROAS targets
  • A weekly report on spend, purchases, cost per result, order value and ROAS

06Strategy

Work out the unit economics first.

07Execution

  • One Scale campaign and one Test campaign, with budget rising only when results hit target.
  • Bundles to lead cold traffic, to lift order value.
  • A cost-per-order target of PKR 690 or less at an order value of PKR 4,500 or more, a ROAS of about 6.5.
  • Tracking for WhatsApp orders, which the reported ROAS could not see.

08Tools and platforms

Meta Ads ManagerGoogle SheetsStore and price review

09Results

The brand got a findings note and an 8-week plan, with ROAS targets that climb toward break-even and a weekly report on spend, purchases, cost per result, order value and ROAS.

10Insights and learnings

ROAS means nothing until you know the margin.

I ask for the margin before I ask for the budget.
