01Project overview

A brand-new packaged flour (atta) brand needed a launch plan: how to spend PKR 4 million over 16 weeks to build demand and win stockists.

02Business background

  • Karachi Division has 20.38 million people (2023 census), in households of 5.4 to 5.9 members.
  • Branded atta is a small share of the category, so loose and stone-ground flour are the real competition.
  • Price research put the brand in the premium band, and its 10 kg pack had no bulk saving.
  • The main premium competitor already claimed 6-hour softness.

03Challenge

Find a claim the brand could own, and a budget that followed distribution instead of running ahead of it.

04Objective

Awareness and trial among families, store visits only where stock is on the shelf, and leads from wholesalers and retailers.

05My role

  • Market and price research
  • Positioning and claims
  • Budget split, channels and campaign types
  • Creative and organic plan
  • Creator scoring
  • Assumptions and estimates, stated openly

06Strategy

  • Led with 8-hour softness and digestion, with one rule: every claim is checked against a document before it goes live.
  • Store ads only where stock is on the shelf, and offers only after client approval.
  • Two options prepared: digital only, and digital plus trade.

07Execution

  • Creatives: 49 ads over 4 months, refreshed every 2 to 3 weeks or when frequency passes 5. Ranked on cost per store-intent click and video completion; the top two scale and the bottom half pause.
  • Organic: 120 posts, 30 a month: 16 reels, 7 carousels and 7 statics. Recipes 25%, family 25%, proof 20%, promotional 20%, engagement 10%.
  • Creators: 17 in 4 tiers, scored on local audience share, real engagement and fake-follower signs, with paid-partnership labels and usage rights.

08Tools and platforms

Meta AdsYouTubeGoogle SearchTikTok testCreator scoring sheetPrice research

09Results

A research-led plan the client could approve line by line, with assumptions stated openly and replaced after the first two-week test.

10Insights and learnings

Digital can't put stock on the shelf.

A launch plan has to follow distribution, not run ahead of it.
