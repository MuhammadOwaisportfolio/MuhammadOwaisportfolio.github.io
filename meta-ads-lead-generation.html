01Project overview

Meta click-to-WhatsApp campaigns for my own business, which supplied custom-printed T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies and staff uniforms to corporate and bulk buyers. I planned, built and paid for every campaign myself, so every rupee was my own.

02Business background

B2B apparel buyers don't check out on a website. They message for a price, because the quote depends on quantity, fabric and the branding method, printing or embroidery. WhatsApp is where the sale starts, so a good campaign is one that starts the right conversations.

03Challenge

I had two video ads and a small budget. My favourite led with colour: a stack of bright fabric. The other opened on the fabric spec, 280 GSM, in the first second. I needed to know which one brought buyers, not just views, before putting more money behind either.

04Objective

  • More WhatsApp enquiries from real buyers.
  • A lower cost per enquiry, proven by a fair test rather than taste.
  • One winning creative to scale.

05My role

Every part of it, as the owner:

  • Campaign structure, audience and budget in Meta Ads Manager
  • Two short product videos and the test design
  • Reading the results and scaling the winner
  • Replying to enquiries, qualifying buyers and quoting

06Strategy

One variable, one decision. Both videos ran on the click-to-WhatsApp objective with equal budget and the same audience, so the only difference was the opening message: colour or spec.

07Execution

  • Ran both videos with equal budget on the click-to-WhatsApp objective.
  • Compared them on hook rate, hold rate and cost per enquiry, not on likes.
  • Scaled the spec-led winner and cut the colour-led ad.
  • Qualified first-time bulk buyers on quantity-based pricing and minimum orders before quoting.

09Results

Across all campaigns the account brought 1,189 WhatsApp enquiries at an average of PKR 13.71. The spec-led creative brought 728 of them at PKR 10.20 each, 43% cheaper than the next-best campaign at PKR 17.98. Enquiries came mostly from men aged 18 to 34 (92% men).

10Insights and learnings

B2B buyers qualify on specs.

The colour-led ad stopped slightly more people, a 26% hook rate against 24%, but the spec-led ad held them longer, 11.01% against 7.98%, and brought enquiries 43% cheaper. The detail that looks boring to a marketer can be the one the buyer is looking for, so I evaluate creatives on cost per enquiry and lead quality, never on the hook alone.
