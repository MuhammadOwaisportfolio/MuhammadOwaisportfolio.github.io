How I work

Five steps on every account.

  1. Ask first

    The business, its margins, its buyers and how leads are handled.

  2. Plan in the open

    Options, targets and assumptions written down before any spend.

  3. Launch lean

    One campaign, one ad set, three ads, with tracking checked.

  4. Optimise by rule

    7 to 14 days of data, then changes by written rules.

  5. Report and scale

    Weekly numbers, bad news first. Budget grows only on target.
