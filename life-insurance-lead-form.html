01Project overview

A life insurer wanted leads for its advisors from Meta, on a limited test budget. I built the lead form and the campaign, then reviewed the lead sheet.

02Business background

Insurance is a high-trust, long-term purchase. Advisors call every lead, so each weak lead costs them time they could spend on a real buyer.

03Challenge

Insurance leads are easy to collect and hard to convert. A cheap, open form fills an advisor's day with people who will never buy.

04Objective

Trade some volume for quality: leads pre-screened on budget and decision maker, at a cost the test budget could sustain.

05My role

  • Lead form design and campaign setup
  • Audience: people aged 25 and above
  • Lead-sheet review and recommendations to the sales team

06Strategy

Ask before you send. Six questions on the form mean an advisor knows the goal, the budget and the decision maker before the first call.

07Execution

  • Six questions before submit: goal, monthly budget, decision maker, city, need and availability for a call.
  • Audience set to people aged 25 and above.
  • Reviewed the lead sheet to see who came through, and what happened after.
