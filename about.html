How I optimise

Rules I work by.

Optimisation rules

  • Pause an ad that has spent about twice the average cost per result with no result.
  • Move 20 to 30% of budget from the weakest ad set to the best one.
  • Scale winners by about 20% every 2 to 3 days.
  • Decide on weekly totals, never on one day.

Lessons from client accounts

  • Location targeting leaks, so exclusions go in on day one.
  • A WhatsApp lead counts only when the message is sent.
  • Showing the price filters out the wrong leads.
  • Real footage beats polished or AI visuals for trust.
  • Markets respond differently, so test one at a time.
